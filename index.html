Minkyu Kim

I am a Ph.D. student at Seoul National University and a member of the Machine Intelligence & Pattern Recognition Laboratory (MIPAL), advised by Prof. Nojun Kwak. My research interests lie in robot learning, embodied AI, and efficient deep learning. I am particularly interested in how embodied systems can learn to interact with the physical world, and how to make the deep learning models behind them more computationally efficient.
